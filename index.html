How many headphones can I use?

Audio Mirror supports up to four additional listening devices while your default Windows output keeps playing.

The number of simultaneous Bluetooth links depends on your adapter, drivers, and headphones. Start with two devices and add more if your hardware supports them.

Can I mix Bluetooth and wired devices?

Yes. Choose the Bluetooth, wired, or USB outputs that appear in Windows. You can mix them in the same setup. Your default Windows output is the source and keeps playing directly.

Will everything be perfectly in sync?

Each device has its own latency. Add 0–400 ms of delay to a faster mirror target to help it match a slower one.

Software cannot remove Bluetooth latency, and precise synchronization is not guaranteed. The controls adjust added delay; they cannot make a slow device play sooner.

What if a device disconnects?

The app shows a reconnecting state and keeps that device selected. It can retry once Windows exposes the audio output again. A change in available devices can briefly restart the audio streams.

If the headphones disconnect in Windows itself, reconnect them there first. Software cannot restore a Bluetooth radio link that Windows has lost.

Which version of Windows do I need?

Windows 10 or Windows 11, 64-bit. Audio Mirror uses Windows audio capture and WebView2 for its interface. Mac, Linux, phones, and tablets are not supported.

How does the payment work?

The download is free. Mirroring requires an account and active access. A USD 2.50 payment through PayPal inside the app adds 30 days. Nothing automatically renews.

Does my audio leave my computer?

Audio routing happens locally on your PC. Internet access is used for account sign-in, access checks, and payments.
